Research Interests

Dr. Anjana S is an Assistant Professor at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, University of Hyderabad. Her research centres on survival analysis and nonparametric inference for lifetime data, spanning competing risks, reliability theory and measurement error models.

Survival Analysis Nonparametric Inference Lifetime Data Analysis Competing Risks Reliability Theory Measurement Error Models
